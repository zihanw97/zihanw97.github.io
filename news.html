Lab updates

Announcements, publications, recognition, and opportunities from the CODE2 Lab.

October 2026

Dr. Wang gave two invited talks at the MS&T conference on continual deep learning and automated machine-learning workflows for improving ReaxFF development. The talks highlighted how adaptive learning and automated computational workflows can accelerate materials modeling and the development of reliable interatomic potentials.

August 2026

Dr. Wang presented her work, “Autonomous Materials Characterization through Continual Deep Learning with Simulated and Experimental Materials Data,” at ASME IDETC-CIE. In this work, she explored how uncertainty-aware learning, simulation-to-experiment adaptation, and continual learning can help enable more reliable and adaptive materials characterization for autonomous scientific discovery.
